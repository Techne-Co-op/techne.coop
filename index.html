01The ground

Three words carry most of what we mean.

They came out of a year of formation meetings, and the room still reaches for them first. Everything else on this site is elaboration.

soil, not a firm

A place where work grows

A firm asserts control conditions on the work inside it. We would rather be soil: provide the conditions for ideas and collaborations to thrive, and let ventures, projects, and friendships grow out of the same ground. The cooperative layer stays deliberately thin — it exists to let people do the right thing, not to do everything for everybody.

“We are trying to think of ourselves as soil where work can emerge.”

scenius

The intelligence of a scene

Genius is one mind; scenius is the collective intelligence that emerges from a community. Cultivating it is one of our stated public benefits, written into the Articles. It is why the record is shared, why the tools are common, and why membership is a matrix rather than a hierarchy: the creative capacity we care about belongs to the room.

“That was our collective intelligence, and all of the work I am doing is informed by it.”

craft

Quality and care

Techne does not limit technology to computers. It is skill, art, technique — ancient technology as much as modern, the craft of neighboring as much as the craft of code. What it demands is care, and care is what you lose under extraction. We orient away from extraction through technology and toward regeneration through it.

“You lose extraction when you bring care into work.”

The quoted lines are from the launch gathering of August 14, 2026, said aloud in the room. The fuller lexicon — seed, venture, program, and the rest — lives in the Almanac.

02The workshop

Four things take shape here, and each feeds the next.

A workshop is judged by what leaves its bench. Four things do here — and each feeds the next, which is what keeps the whole alive.

01 · the hands

The people

Relationships form the old way — around real work, at the bench and at gatherings, over time. A community of practice: newcomers learn beside experienced hands, and belonging grows with skill.

feeds → the practice
02 · the method

The practice

Craft is learned by making, not watching. Courses, daily rituals, and shared work hold the work to the standard of craft — augmentation, not automation — and you bring your own craft into the room.

feeds → the work
03 · the made

The work

What members make joins a common record — added to, never erased, each piece carrying its maker’s name, the way a workshop signs what leaves its bench. Single efforts become a body of work none of us could make alone.

feeds → the tools
04 · the instruments

The tools

We build our tools the way a workshop should: for ourselves first, used daily, then shared. The record, the publishing, the infrastructure underneath stay a commons, owned by the members who use them. No streaks, no scores, no engagement mechanics — instruments that serve the work rather than mine the attention.

feeds → the people

The workshop keeps a quiet ecological grammar for what it makes: a seed is one contributor’s tending, a venture is two or more hands committed, a program is a habitat the whole cooperative sustains.

03In the open

Watch it form.

This site is not a brochure for a finished thing. It is the working record of a cooperative in formation, published while it forms — drafts labeled as drafts, contradictions reported rather than smoothed over, every adoption traceable to the people who made it.
