The ways in

Techne is owned and governed by the people who use it, and there is more than one way to use it. Four classes of participation stand, on two tracks: an access track you can join today, at the hub in Boulder or in the guild from anywhere, and a patron track you grow into. Start wherever fits, and step deeper as it makes sense. Nothing here asks for ownership on day one.

Three doors enroll openly across two classes: no invitation, no buy-in, cancel on reasonable notice. All are ways of belonging to the workshop without the obligations of governance, and time spent here counts. How participation becomes ownership the two pathways

At a desk: a Co-working Member who reaches a board-established patronage threshold may be invited to apply for Cooperative Member status. Upon Board acceptance, the member executes the Membership Agreement, pays the $100 one-time share, and moves fully to the patron track. The credits earned as a Co-working Member inform the invitation; the threshold and the credit methodology are set by board resolution and specified in Schedule A of the Participation Framework.

In the guild, and anywhere on the access track: after ninety days of continuous participation in good standing, a participant becomes invitable. When two Cooperative Members have each recorded an invitation in the cooperative's record, the participant is eligible to apply. An invitation is a member's act, not a form's checkbox: it says a member knows this person's work and direction of travel. Board acceptance, the Membership Agreement, and the $100 share follow exactly as above. Eligibility opens the door and never pushes anyone through it; membership is chosen from both sides.
