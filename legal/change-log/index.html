§2

The structure this log adopts, unchanged from the indexes

Each index is a table of entries with four columns: ID, Title, Section, Nature, where Nature draws from a fixed vocabulary (Blocking, High priority, Advisable, Clarifying, Extends, New, Affirmed, Diverges). Each ID anchors a detail block below the table carrying a Problem statement and a Proposed change with the from-text and the to-text. Cross-references are inline links by ID. This log keeps that shape so an entry reads the same wherever it sits.

Added for the pre-ratification period
append only
A wrong entry is superseded by a new entry saying so. Nothing here is edited away.
three fields on every detail block
Directed by, Authority, and Evidence. An entry whose only evidence is a page this estate wrote about itself is marked UNEVIDENCED until a minute or a signed record exists.
identifiers continue the existing series
BL-nn for the Bylaws, MA-nn for the Membership Agreement, and a new series where an instrument needs one: PF-nn for the Participation Framework, GT-nn for the Guild Participation Terms, LOG-nn for this log. The indexes end at BL-A07 and MA-A04, so numbered entries here begin at BL-09 and MA-08.

§5

Standing note on the ratification record

The June versus July ratification conflict, logged as the open reading at DOC-01·08, is answered, if the steward's statement of 2026-08-19 holds, by the finding that no final ratification has occurred at all. The published record is split on it. The techne.institute legal index says of Bylaws v.2: "Ratified by board vote, June 2026. Legally in effect." The techne.institute summary page says: "The Bylaws and Membership Agreement are v.2.1 drafts, not yet ratified, not yet binding on any member." Both fetched 2026-08-19. Both are pages this estate published about its own work, so neither is evidence of what the board did.

This estate's own legal shelf takes neither side: it carries the Bylaws as Drafted and states that the accounts of the mid-2026 verbal board action do not agree and that no minute supplies any of them. That is the honest position and this log does not disturb it. The answer enters the evidence chain only when a board minute recites it. Until then, every entry that depends on the Bylaws being a draft carries that dependency on its face, and whichever way the recital settles it, the page that loses must be corrected and the correction logged here.
