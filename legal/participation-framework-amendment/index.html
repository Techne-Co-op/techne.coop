A.1

What this instrument amends, and the honest limits of that

This instrument amends the Participation Framework, the Layer 3 patronage instrument described on the legal shelf as governing how members participate in cooperative Programs and how contributions accrue recognition.

Disclosure the drafter owes the board. The Framework's text is not published on techne.coop, the shelf says so expressly, and it was not found anywhere in the repository. Its adoption status is contested in the record: the legal shelf lists it as Drafted; the ledger, packet U-10, calls it the ratified Participation Framework; the GUILD module description has it adopted by board resolution on June 24, 2026. June 24, 2026 is also the date whose summary notes contradict themselves about whether Bylaws ratification occurred (DOC-01·08). The drafter therefore cannot quote the text this amendment changes, and no quotation appears below. This amendment is drafted additively: it adds a new section and changes no existing sentence.

Fallback construction. Because the Framework's status is uncertain, this instrument is drafted to operate on either footing. If the Framework stands adopted, this is Amendment No. 1 to it, adding the section in Part B. If the Framework is not adopted, this stands alone as a resolution of the Board of Directors establishing the Guild Participant level and its terms, under the authority the Bylaws already reserve to the Board. Either way the operative text is the same and one board vote adopts it.

Part B

Operative text, proposed

RESOLVED, that the Participation Framework is amended by adding the following as a new section, numbered by the Secretary to follow the Framework's last section, and that, if and to the extent the Participation Framework is found not to have been adopted, the following stands as a policy resolution of the Board with the same force:

1. Establishment. There is established within Class Three (Community Participant, Bylaws 1.1(c)) a participation level called the Guild Participant level (working name; the public name is election GUILD-D5). The guild is the virtual way into the Techne craft-guild Program: a network of self-organizing communities of practice, open to participation from anywhere without presence in Boulder.

2. Not admission to membership. Enrollment as a Guild Participant is a contractual program relationship and is not admission to membership in the Cooperative. A Guild Participant is not admitted as a patron member or investor member within the meaning of C.R.S. section 7-58-102(13), makes no contribution within the meaning of C.R.S. section 7-58-102(5), purchases no share of stock under Bylaws 1.2.4, executes no Membership Agreement, holds no member's interest, no capital account, no governance rights, and no financial rights (C.R.S. section 7-58-102(9), (10)). The Cooperative shall not allocate profits or losses to, or make distributions to, Guild Participants as such, and shall not adopt the non-member allocation option of C.R.S. section 7-58-305(1)(f) for the guild.

3. Dues. Guild dues are payable monthly at one of four tiers, self-chosen by the Participant: ten dollars ($10), twenty five dollars ($25), one hundred dollars ($100), or two hundred dollars ($200) per month, as published at techne.coop/participation. Every tier opens the same doors; no tier purchases governance, priority, or standing over any other Participant. Dues are dues, never capital, at any tier: no payment under this level is a capital contribution, opens a capital account, or is credited toward any membership share.

4. Terms. The rights and obligations of Guild Participants are stated in the Guild Participation Terms, adopted by Board resolution and versioned on the legal shelf. The Terms may not contradict this section; where they do, this section controls.

5. Pathway. Continuous guild participation in good standing counts toward eligibility for invitation to Cooperative Member (Class One) on the same footing as presence at the hub, per the invitation pathway in the Terms. Nothing in this section or the Terms creates a right to membership; membership arrives only by invitation, Board acceptance, execution of the Membership Agreement, and payment of the membership share.

6. Elections recorded at adoption. The Board records with this amendment: GUILD-D2, the invitation count (two drafted; three available); GUILD-D5, the public name of the level.

Part C

Matters flagged for counsel, not resolved here

1 · the word "member" in Bylaws 1.1(c)
The Bylaws call a Community Participant "a member." This amendment establishes the guild inside that class while providing that a Guild Participant is not admitted as a member under C.R.S. section 7-58-102(13). Colorado's definition controls what membership means at law, and 7-58-502(1)(a) says a person becomes a member as provided in the articles or bylaws. A root fix is in the same packet: Pre-Ratification Revision No. 1. If it is adopted, this flag narrows to a conformity check. If not, the guild's protection remains that no admission act, no stock purchase, and no contribution occurs; counsel should confirm that suffices.
2 · securities characterization
Dues buy program access and services with no profit share, no distribution, and no expectation of return from the efforts of others; the design intent is that no tier is a security or an investment contract. That conclusion is counsel's to give, not the drafter's.
3 · the Framework's own amendment clause
Unread, because the text could not be found. If the Framework surfaces with a stricter clause, re-adoption under it.
4 · organizational-window authority
C.R.S. section 7-58-304 against Bylaws Article XII before any member exists, should the board ever want a true Bylaws amendment.
5 · tax treatment of dues revenue
The counting rules mark adjacent questions as being for tax counsel. No CPA or tax counsel is engaged.
