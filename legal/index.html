Where the cooperative stands

RegenHub, LCA has been called to order. The Articles are filed with the Colorado Secretary of State, the board was called to order in its special meeting of August 14, 2026 and confirmed on August 19, 2026, and its officers are in office: Aaron Gabriel, President; Todd Youngblood, Secretary; Lucian Hymer, Treasurer. The Bylaws and Membership Agreement are adopted and authorized by the board, and formation counsel has recognized the cooperative's standing to admit members: a person signs the membership agreement, pays the buy-in, and the cooperative is running. Their published texts still carry the Drafted mark, because member ratification under Article 12 is anticipated, not yet held, and no member has been admitted. One board seat, of the seven the organizers' consent elected, remains open. Nothing anywhere in this record carries the Ratified mark, because nothing has been adopted by a member vote.

We publish in that state deliberately. A cooperative whose members arrive after every decision is already made is not one anybody co-creates, so this remains the working record of a live formation, and it will keep changing as ratification and the board vacancy are resolved.

Read it accordingly. A page describing how something works is describing how it is meant to work; it binds when an instrument is executed, a board resolution passes, or the members vote. Each document carries its own status mark, on this page or on the intranet shelf. Where a page anywhere on this site and this notice disagree, this notice is right.

Defects found in the drafts after they were published are logged openly at Corrections and open readings, including the ones this estate introduced itself. What the claims audits found and did not correct, and why, is reported at The audit remainder.
