On the name

To The board and organizers, RegenHub, LCA

From Todd Youngblood, Ventures and Operations Steward

Date August 20, 2026

Subject Why I would support renaming Gitcoin to Techne Foundation

The ask

Kevin has floated renaming Gitcoin to Techne Foundation, alongside domesticating the entity as a US 501(c)(3), and asked whether this cooperative would support it. I would. Here is why, before it gets smoothed into a pitch.

The layer, not the weather

The tension worth building an institution around is not technology but the ownership and control of it. Technology is a weather system you can only have opinions about; ownership and control is a layer, and a layer can be examined, measured, and changed. Look at the layer and our competing pictures of the economy stop being rival descriptions and become outcomes with a site of production.

Technology is also not a sector. It enters at every boundary, from the living planet through extraction, manufacture, service, and knowledge work, so concentration at the ownership layer compounds everywhere rather than in one place. That is what techne names: craft before industry, belonging to the potter as much as to the engineer, and so does the problem. Every practice, from an art to a science to the tending of land, depends on instruments. A foundation named for craft names the layer instead of claiming a sector.

The program the name follows from

A name earns itself by scoping work, so let me state the work: the accounting that communities of practice do not have. The firm is evaluated through financial statements, periodic second-order abstractions; underneath them sits the first-order reality of what was made, moved, and transformed, by whom, with what. McCarthy's resources-events-agents framework (1982) describes that layer, and Engelbart's H-LAM/T the complementary one: REA sees the exchange, H-LAM/T the capability. Together they let a cooperative, a trust, or an association state what it did in terms a member can read, which the corporate instrument was not built to do. Choosing well among those forms needs a lexicon the conversation does not have, and building it is the foundation's work, oriented by Ostrom and the commons literature. That is the program, and techne is the category that scopes it.

Why this makes no villains

Concentration of control is an arrangement, not a conspiracy, and it outlives every individual inside it, so blame is unfair and useless. Aiming at the ownership layer yields a critique of the corporate form that lays no fault on individuals and creates no victims. That is not politeness; it is what keeps the work legible to people on every side of it, and a 501(c)(3) educational rather than adversarial.

What I am asking for, and what I am not

  1. Support for the name in principle, as our answer to Kevin. Not funds, staff, or our mark.
  2. Acknowledgment that neither Kevin nor I is disinterested. RegenHub registered techne.coop as a Colorado trade name on August 12, 2026. The relationship between a Techne Foundation and this cooperative should be said out loud.
  3. Deference on clearance. Kevin is running the trademark search; our registration is a fact it needs.

The ownership and control of technology is the conversation civic society is not having. I would rather we be on the record supporting an institution named for the whole of craft than watch the naming go somewhere narrower.

Todd Youngblood · Ventures and Operations Steward, RegenHub, LCA
