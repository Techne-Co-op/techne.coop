parked · waits on the counting rules
See your contribution history, itemized.
See your capital account as a fold over that history. Balances are computed from the event log, never stored as editable numbers.
Follow any figure to the events and the policy that produced it.
What this door waits on, by name. The Board adopting the counting rules: which contributions count, how each kind is valued, and when the first accounting year begins. The rules are drafted and staged at COUNTING-RULES v1; they bind on adoption, and this page opens when they do. Nothing here is withheld by an administrator; the numbers are absent because the rules that would compute them are not yet the cooperative's act.

Already true underneath

The substrate has carried capital events, valued events, and the account fold since the eleven laws landed: one voting share per voting member, each account equal to the fold of its member's events, nothing edited, corrections as compensating entries. The walkaway rehearsal proves the fold round-trips. What is missing is not machinery; it is the adopted rules that give the machinery its numbers.

The frame of it is written at Program Patronage, and the weights stay with the Plan.
