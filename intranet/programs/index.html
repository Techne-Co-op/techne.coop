Programs

The cooperative's organized life, arranged for engagement. A Program is an organizing body for the Opportunities surfaced in the commons: a purpose, a Coordinator, and a ratified way of recognizing what the work is worth. Not every Opportunity has one, and nothing requires it.

Designate a Program

A steward or director act (Bylaws §3.1, §4.1). The Program enters the record as an agent and its designation event together, standing in formation until the board adopts its POLICY-PATRONAGE instrument, which is what brings a Coordinator and a way of recognizing work.
