First time here?
Take three steps, in this order. Open the shelf and read the agreements that bind you. Open the calendar and sign up for something. Open the board and find work or play you want in on. Everything else is in the menu on the left.
What this page is
Your front page as a member. It is a set of links into the cooperative's shared record: one database that every page here reads from and writes to, so what you see is what the cooperative knows. Nothing is stored twice and nothing is changed behind your back.
What you can do here
  • Read the agreements you are bound by, and sign the ones that need your signature.
  • Look up other members, sign up for gatherings, and answer open calls for work or play.
  • Check your own account, and follow the running count of what the cooperative has recorded.
Words used in a special sense here are defined in the Lexicon.
