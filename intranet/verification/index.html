The verification walk

The same cards as the public run-book, walked here instead of spoken into a recording. Read a card, do its steps, click the verdict, type the one sentence. When you stop, the page writes the record of what you walked, and that record is what moves the marks.

What this page does and does not do. It moves nothing. Your verdicts live in this browser and nowhere else: no verdict is written to the cooperative’s record by clicking it, and closing the tab on a different machine loses nothing because nothing was sent. A mark moves when a person opens the pull request that carries this walk’s record, and the ledger wins every disagreement with this page. The verdict is yours, under your name and the date you give it.
