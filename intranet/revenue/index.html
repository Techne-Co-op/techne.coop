Revenue

The cooperative's recurring revenue, composed rather than flattened: each Program offers named monthly subscription tiers, each tier's active count is a reading from the system that holds it, and the figure at the top is the sum of the latest reading per tier. No figure here is entered on this page; every line names its source, its basis, and its read time. A reading is an observation beside the record, never a movement in it; Xero remains the book of account and the treasury remains where movement and meaning meet.

The registry

The steward's surface for the tier registry: what each Program offers, declared as an act in the record. Declaring a tier writes the row and its revenue.tier_declared event together; retiring one leaves every reading standing. Counts and readings never enter here; they arrive by the runner, read-only from the systems that hold them.
