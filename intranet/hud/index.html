What this surface refuses

Read-only, always. Nothing here writes anywhere; selecting and reading produce no event.

No person is rendered. No individual counts, no per-author figures, no ranking, in any view, ever.

No analytics on its own readers. The page gathers nothing about who views it.

No untraceable figure. Every number is computed by scripts/hud_cut.py from the public repository; a reader who distrusts a figure can recompute the cut with no key.

No score. Counts and named states only; no percentage of completion, no progress bar, no maturity index.

No unattributed frame. Interpretive sentences are marked as the instrument’s reading, never as the cooperative’s position.
