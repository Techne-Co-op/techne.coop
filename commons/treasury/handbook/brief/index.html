§7

Five for the board, five for you

Only the board can close: TREASURY-POLICY adoption; COUNTING-RULES adoption; Q3, redeemability; the LABOR-SCHEDULE entry; the treasurer election formalities.

The treasurer's duties, per the record: propose disbursements; execute within bands, post-adoption; attest reconciliation windows (G-T: a movement shown settled equals a movement traced by hand); confirm non-program Contributions (COUNTING-RULES §3); custody of the statements runner output. One clock runs: the treasury committee has 90 days from first member sign-up for COUNTING-RULES (staged), LABOR-SCHEDULE (the hole), TREASURY-POLICY (parked), and the payout, cash-out, and reinvestment schedule (unstarted).

Everything else, the ledger bed T-01 through T-07, the statements store, the open-accounting trajectory, and the live figures, is in the Handbook.
