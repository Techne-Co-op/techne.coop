§2

Movement and meaning

the core principle, TREASURY §4

The whole design rests on one separation, stated in TREASURY §4: "treasury records movement; patronage records meaning; neither substitutes for the other, and the pairing of the two is the trustworthy fact." A member's cash contribution is two events in two layers: a treasury fact that dollars arrived with a bank reference, and a patronage fact that a capital account was credited under a policy version, each carrying the other's id. When the layers disagree, that disagreement is a feature: a movement with no meaning, or a meaning with no movement, is exactly what the reconciliation tally exists to surface.

§3

The money loop

rails, reads, statements, log, tally

Money moves on external rails; the record reads them; humans move the money. The rails connected as of 2026-08-04 are Stripe at intake, Mercury as bank custody, Xero as the book of account, and a Safe onchain, every connection read-only. The statements runner reads all four, renders monthly statements into the statements store at the member cut, and writes nothing to the event log. The governed movements themselves, once the treasury verbs migrate, land as events in the append-only log, and the state anyone trusts is a fold over those events: state = fold(events).

the money loop · reads above, record below · no system moves money; humans execute at the bank

Hold the boundary firmly: execution is manual at the bank. No system holds a key or initiates a movement; the record describes movement and humans execute it. The draft policy pins this in its payload as execution="manual_at_bank", and TREASURY defers programmatic initiation to a proposal that does not exist.

§4

The signature discipline

TREASURY-POLICY v1.1, draft

The draft policy, staged to the FSC and board and adopted by nobody, sets a floor of two distinct human acts on every disbursement, and bands by amount above it. The roles: propose is the treasurer or the steward; approve is a director or officer, never the proposer; execute is the treasurer or a named director; attest is the treasurer. Standing purposes, inside band 1 limits: a dues refund; a documented reimbursement against a receipt; a tax-covering allocation distribution.

§9

The statements store

0019_treasury_statements.sql, applied live

One table, treasury_statements: a month column, text and unique in the form YYYY-MM, and a payload column, jsonb. Statements are outside observations, not log facts: rendering one writes nothing to events. The member cut carries no member names, no member ids, and no per-member amounts, with small-cohort withholding guarding the seam between aggregate and identity. Select is admitted by RLS to every authenticated member; writes travel only through the management channel under steward custody. Three monthly statements, May through July 2026, are rendered and live, each provisional until a reconciliation window attests its month.

§10

Open accounting, real and theoretical

the trajectory, honestly labeled

The build's trajectory is open accounting: books legible to members and, in time, to the public the cooperative claims to benefit, balanced against member privacy by design rather than by discretion. Keep the two columns distinct.

Real, in the record now: the read-only rails of §8; monthly member-cut statements; the append-only event log with its tally, state = fold(events); the two-hands rule, proposer never approver; attested reconciliation windows as the standard (G-T); and the discipline that every figure cites its instrument, event, or read.

Theoretical, staged or anticipated: the four-band signature discipline, in force only on adoption; the two-layer movement-and-meaning pairing, live once patronage events land; capital accounts in the Subchapter K sense, IRC 704(b) context, where the 40/30/20/10 allocation formula remains the FSC's to finalize and the weights live in the Plan, not in COUNTING-RULES; and public-facing cuts beyond the member cut, a future board decision under the open benefit report.

Privacy design: the cut discipline, member cut against steward cut; aggregates only; small-cohort withholding; no per-member amounts on shared surfaces; drafts noindexed.

the tax boundary

The tax character of intake, dues versus capital versus program payment, is routed to a CPA and tax counsel (TREASURY §14). No CPA is engaged yet. A counsel memo is staged at /accounting/counsel-memo/. Do not resolve tax character questions from this page or any page; route them.
