What is in force, and what waits

This record is open to read while it is still being built. Pages will change, and every page says whether a thing is adopted or only drafted. If something reads wrong, is missing, or contradicts another page, say so: open an issue on the public repository and name the page and the sentence. Corrections from readers are part of how this gets finished.

The instruments on the left are in force and the surfaces built on them can be walked today. What is on the right is built, marked, and waiting on an act no software performs: an adoption by the board, a signature by the person it binds, or a named person applying a change to the live record. Nothing is left off this list because it is unfinished. It is on the list because it is.

What it is

Scholars and craftspeople have kept commonplace books for centuries: not an archive, but a working tool. You copy in what governs your practice, what you've agreed to, what has happened and what it led to. The entries are organized for use rather than display. When someone needs to understand the whole, they open the book.

RegenHub's Commonplace Book is that practice carried into cooperative life. It holds the authoritative state of the cooperative: who the members are, what agreements bind them, what events have taken place, and what each person's share of the work and the results amounts to. Every figure can be traced to the events that produced it. Every rule can be traced to the governing document that established it. Nothing is hidden behind a decision no one can follow back to its source.

The book is not an app for doing the work. It is the layer that remembers what has been done — so that the next question has an honest answer, and the next member doesn't have to ask.

Words that carry weight here are defined in one place, the Lexicon, and the documents that govern the whole are the Common Record Series.

Where its words came from

Before there was a record to keep there were fifteen conversations, between August 2025 and February 2026, in which a coworking space talked itself into becoming a cooperative. The Commonplace is what was said in them: seventy-nine entries gathered from the meeting summaries and arranged by what an idea does rather than by when it appeared.

Nearly every term this book operates on entered there, and can be read back to the afternoon it arrived. Soil rather than firm, which dissolved a whole class of accounting anxiety in one metaphor. Qualification over quantification, which is why contributions are made visible here without being priced. Trust rather than reputation. Pace layering, which is why the articles, the bylaws, and the membership agreement are meant to change at three different speeds. Augmentation over automation, which is the position the studio takes on its own tools.

That book is a formation record and it governs nothing. It binds no one, and where it and this record disagree, this record is what is true. The steward asks that it be read as the lens the rest of the work is perceived through, which is a different kind of authority than an instrument carries, and a useful one: this page holds what is true now, and that one holds what was meant.

How it is steered

The book is not only a record. It is the sensing half of a system that steers itself, and the vocabulary says so on purpose. Cybernetics takes its name from the Greek for steersman, the same root as the word governor. A garden under a gardener's hand is a steered system: sensing and tending, in loops.

Read against the viable system frame the cooperative already works in, the book and its surfaces lay out so.

Operations · one
The four capabilities and their beds: the work members live inside. Belonging, gathering, finding, sharing.
Coordination · two
The Ground beneath the beds, and the one frame every surface wears, so the beds do not collide.
Audit · three
The verification suites and the probe matrix: the channel by which the system checks that it agrees with itself, on every change, without being asked.
Intelligence · four
The signal loop. What is workable, what unblocks the most, what has stalled quietly are exactly the intelligence questions, so the instrument keeps its name.
Identity · five
Proofs and adoption: the acts that close the loop between the machine's claims and the cooperative's word. Nothing becomes real until a person adopts it.
The algedonic channel
Stop cards. Pain routed past everything, straight to attention, in one shape that anyone may raise.
The transducer
The tally: the reading by which the system knows its own state from its own history. State at any moment is the tally of everything that happened up to it, recomputed and never stored.

This is not a metaphor laid over software. Every row above names something that exists in the record: a bed, a suite, a card, a view, a database trigger that refuses to let history be edited. The reading is set out in full in the Lexicon, and the plan it governs is the Almanac.

Where a member reads it

A record no one can open is a filing cabinet. The intranet is where a member reads and writes this one, behind sign-in, because some of what the book holds is nobody else's business.

It is a set of doors into a single shared record rather than a set of apps: the shelf for what binds you, the calendar for showing up, the board for finding work and play, the Programs that organize that work, your share for what your contribution amounts to, the Desk for the treasury's governed movements, and direction for setting the cooperative's instrument working on the record. Nothing is kept twice and nothing is quietly overwritten. Every figure on those surfaces is a tally computed at the moment you ask, so it can be followed back to the events that produced it.

This page and the ones it links to are the public half of the same book. Both halves read the same record.

How it federates

A posture note rather than a doctrine: where this record stands toward systems beyond it, written down so nobody has to ask. The record rides signed events. An entry is written once, carries its provenance, and is never edited, which is what lets it travel without losing its authority. How fine a citation each claim carries when it travels is itself doctrine, stated in the grain of the record.

It stands in two rings. The inner ring is operational: a membership relay the cooperative operates, where owner-attested keys speak and the full record lives. The outer ring is public, and today it is this web estate, the pages you are reading. What crosses from one ring to the other is not decided page by page. The Egress Doctrine states the rule: whose the record is, whose the public digest is, and the written rule over rights class and content class that decides which subset departs. The doctrine stands drafted, and it binds only when a person with standing adopts it.

A wider outer commons on the AT Protocol is a candidate under consideration, and it is not adopted. Naming a candidate is not taking the step; if the cooperative ever takes it, the taking will be a recorded act, and the same egress rule will govern what is published there. The public anchors for this posture are issue #217 and issue #218. What this section states as one posture among many, the federation vision states at length on the public estate: what federation means here, what the cooperative commits to, and what it invites.

Agents hold identity in this posture the way section 10 of the build instructions states: an agent is a keypair, owner-attested to a named human, its drafting waiting on a person's adoption, and any seat in another organization's workspace a configuration act requiring both owners and a named human on this side. None exists today.

What has been grafted in

The book grows by graft. A module is a document the cooperative adopts, and adoption brings its work into the plan under the same marks as everything else.

  • Program Patronage adopted 2026-07-22
    Programs as organizing bodies for the opportunities on the board: contributions, claims, and the allocation sequence. Its counting rules are the instrument the Share bed waits on. Read the module.
  • Treasury adopted 2026-07-22
    The movement layer over the cooperative's rails, governed movements recorded as events, reconciliation as a tally. The Desk stands and reads, and stays read-only until the board adopts the treasury policy. Read the module.
  • Common Agency grafted 2026-07-27, proposed and not formally adopted
    A patron member may give the cooperative's instrument work as a recorded act, within a declared scope, with every artifact under two hands. The rail and the desk are built; the bed waits on one decision, whether a non-human agent may hold a scoped authority in the record at all. Read the module.
  • Work Standing and Guild Participation drafted, not adopted
    Two modules staged with their beds recorded at anticipated: how work earns its standing as it matures, and a virtual way in for people who are not in the room. Neither binds until the cooperative adopts it, and the pages say so. Read Work Standing.
  • Governance drafted, no bed minted
    The machinery by which the cooperative's bodies act: the two modes, the instruments and the hands that hold them, the acts and the refusals they carry, and the minute as a projection of acts rather than an account of a room. Drafted for review, with eight decisions staged and nothing entered in the ledger. Read the module.
