Catalog of Living Intelligences

You arrive with a title. So this catalog now opens where you do: at the role, because the role is how collective organization currently composes intelligence.

Beneath the titles sit seventeen questions a body of work needs someone to carry. No common role carries one alone. Each title is a bundle: it holds one question as home, the thing it is actually accountable for, and reaches into a few others without owning them. That composition is what an org chart is, read honestly.

Each card below is an archetype: the role as it commonly exists, the intelligence it holds, the intelligences it reaches into, and the limit, which is the honest part: what the title does not carry, however senior it sounds. Find your own card first, and read its limit before anything else.

The seventeen, indexed

The intelligences remain the substance beneath the titles. Every role above names exactly one home; every intelligence is homed by at least two roles. The fewer its homes, the thinner the ground it stands on when someone leaves.

In each sigil the seventeen intelligences stand in five family rows: desirability, viability, feasibility, legitimacy, continuity. The solid cell is the role's home; the half-lit cells are its reaches; the empty cells are questions the title does not visit. Nothing here writes back to the record.
