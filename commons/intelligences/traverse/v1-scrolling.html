A second lens on the Catalog of Living Intelligences

The Traverse

A corporate title names one question out of seventeen and calls it a career. This page starts from the title you were given, shows exactly what it perceives and what it cannot, and then walks you out of it. Nothing here writes to any record.

i Enter as a title

Pick the role you hold, or the one you were last hired as. The catalog knows fifty-four.

ii Dissolve

The title decomposes into intelligences: one held as home, a few reached into. The rest of the field goes dark. The dark is not empty, it is specific: each unlit cell is a question this title has no standing to answer.

iii Traverse

Roles are not boxes, they are edges. Any title that shares one of your lit intelligences is one step away, and its reach becomes borrowable. Choose a dark intelligence and see the shortest path from where you stand to someone who holds it as home.

iv The thin ground

v Compose

Now drop the title. Select the intelligences you actually carry, whatever your contract says, and see whether any role in the catalog names that shape. Usually none does. That gap is not a defect in you, it is the resolution limit of titles.
