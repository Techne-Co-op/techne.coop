Agreements the board originates

The shelf records that a contract was signed. It does not record the board act that authorized anyone to sign it, and under the Bylaws that act is where a contract begins.

§1

What is missing

An agreement can only reach the shelf by a migration. A build agent writes the row, a human adopts the pull request, and the instrument appears. That is adequate for the governing documents, which are few and change rarely. It is wrong for contracts, which the cooperative will make continuously and which no build agent should be in the path of.

The second gap is sharper, and it is the one that matters. The bed records execution: a signature, its date, and now the capacity it was made in. It records nothing about authorization. Under Bylaws Article VII §7.1 the Board may authorize an officer or agent to enter into a contract in the name of the Cooperative, and that authority may be general or confined to a specific instance. A contract that is signed without that authorization is not a contract the cooperative made. The record as it stands cannot tell the two apart.

So the question is not how to add a row. It is how the shelf holds the chain the Bylaws already describe: the Board authorizes, an officer executes, the instrument stands.

§2

Two facts, held apart

2.1 the authorization

A board act naming what may be entered into and by whom. It is dated, it is minuted, and it is either general (an officer may execute agreements of a class) or confined to a specific instance (this statement of work, with this counterparty, up to this amount). The shelf should hold a reference to the resolution and the scope it granted, not a copy of the minutes.

Where a director or officer has an interest in the transaction, §17.1 requires the material facts to be disclosed and the authorization to carry a majority of disinterested directors. That is a fact about the authorization, and it belongs on the authorization, not on the signature.

2.2 the execution

What the bed already holds, extended by B-08: who signed, when the record was written, the date they attest to, and the capacity. §4.2 makes the President the default executing officer; §7.1 lets the Board place that elsewhere. The execution cites the authorization it rests on, or it stands unauthorized and the surface says so plainly.

2.3 what the shelf may show

An agreement in negotiation is not an agreement that may be published. B-08 handled this once, by hand, for the Gitcoin statement of work: no uri, no body, name and standing only. Doing it by hand does not scale and a rule obeyed by hand is unaudited. Disclosure should be a property of the row, set when the row is made, so that the estate cannot publish terms nobody authorized it to publish.

§3

User journeys

Five journeys, each stated as the person walking it would state it, each with the condition under which it is accepted. An acceptance line that cannot be checked is not an acceptance line.

boardAuthorize a contractWe resolve that the cooperative may enter a statement of work with a named counterparty, and that a named officer may execute it. Accepted when an authorization record exists carrying the date of the board act, the scope granted (general or a specific instance), the officer or agent authorized, and a citation to the minute in which it was taken, and an agreement.authorized event is recorded.
secretaryOriginate the instrumentI want to put an agreement on the shelf myself, without a migration and without an engineer, so that the record keeps pace with what the board actually decided. Accepted when a director or officer creates an agreement row from the surface, naming its code, title, version, kind, and disclosure, and no build agent and no schema change is in the path.
secretaryWithhold the termsI want an instrument still in negotiation to appear on the shelf by name and standing without its text, so that members can see it exists without the estate disclosing a transaction that has not travelled. Accepted when the row carries its disclosure setting explicitly, a row set to name-and-standing renders with no body and no link on every surface that reads it, and raising the disclosure is a distinct recorded act by a named human.
presidentExecute what was authorizedI want my signature to cite the board act that let me sign, so that the record shows the cooperative made this contract rather than that I did. Accepted when a signature made in an officer capacity carries the authorization it rests on, an execution offered against an agreement with no authorization is refused or marked unauthorized on its face, and the refusal names §7.1.
memberRead the chainI want to open a contract the cooperative has made and see, in order, that the board authorized it, who executed it, and on what date, so that I can tell an act of the cooperative from an act of a person. Accepted when an active member opens the agreement and sees the authorization, its scope, the execution, and the interested-director disclosure where §17.1 applied, with any element that is absent shown as absent rather than omitted.
§4

The deliverable, and why it is not written yet

The deliverable of this piece is a migration in the shape below. It is named here and not written, because three of its four parts are stop-card matters under the Build Protocol and one of them touches governance semantics directly.

the tableagreement_authorizations: the board act, its date, its scope, the officer or agent authorized, the minute cited, and the disinterested-majority fact where §17.1 applied. One agreement may rest on more than one, since authority may be granted and later confined.
the columnagreements.disclosure: what the estate may show. At minimum name-and-standing, members-only, and public. Set at origination, raised only by a recorded act. This generalizes the hand-made decision in B-08.
the functionoriginate_agreement, a definer function parallel in shape to sign_agreement (0003) and comment_on_agreement (0025), granted to directors and officers only, which writes the row, writes its event, and links them atomically.
the linksignatures.authorization_id, so an execution cites the board act it rests on. Nullable, because instruments already on the shelf predate the concept and the record should not pretend otherwise.

Each row-level policy will carry a comment naming its bylaw anchor, per the Build Protocol; the anchors are §7.1 for who may originate, §4.2 for who may execute, §17.1 for the disclosure fact, and §1.2.9 for who may read. A policy without an anchor does not merge.

§5

Stop cards

Three, one question each.

standing inB-09 §2.3found: disclosure is currently a property of how a row was hand-written, not a property of the row. The question: does agreements.disclosure enter the Information Model as a first-class field, or does the estate keep withholding terms by convention? Default taken: first-class field, because a rule obeyed by hand is unaudited and drifts. proposal
standing inB-09 §3found: the board has six directors in office and one vacant seat, and no board act on the record has authorized any contract. The question: may an agreement be originated in-system before a board resolution exists, marked unauthorized, or must the authorization come first? Default taken: origination is allowed and renders as unauthorized on its face, because the record should be able to hold a draft the board has not yet reached. proposal
standing inB-09 §4found: this piece grants a write capability to directors and officers that the Authority Map does not currently carry. The question: who approves it, given that the Build Protocol makes authority and governance semantics a Tier B review at least? Default taken: Tier B, organizer review plus a decision record, and the Authority Map matrix gains its rows only when the migration is applied. proposal

§6

What this does not do

The bounds, stated so that the next piece does not have to guess them.

No counterparty signingA party outside the cooperative still cannot sign through the system. That needs an agent row and a membership question B-08 already declined to answer, and this piece declines it too.
No minute writingThe authorization cites a minute. It does not create one. Minutes are the secretary’s, taken under the minute protocol, and the shelf points at them.
No moneyAn authorization may name an amount. Nothing here moves, holds, or reconciles funds; the treasury bed governs that and is held on its own conditions.
No quorum arithmeticWhether a given board act carried is decided by the people in the room and recorded in the minute. The shelf records what the minute says, and does not recompute it.
the working agreement of this piece

A signature proves that a person signed. It does not prove that the cooperative agreed. The Bylaws already hold those apart, at Article VII and at Article IV, and a record that collapses them is telling members something the instruments do not say. This piece makes the shelf keep the distinction the cooperative is already governed by.
