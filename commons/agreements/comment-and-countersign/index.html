Comment and countersign

The agreements bed gains two acts: a recorded remark on an instrument, and a signature that carries the date the signer attests to.

§1

What was missing

The bed could hold a versioned instrument and record that a member had signed it. It could not hold what anyone said about the instrument before signing, and it could not record the date a signer attests to, only the timestamp the row was written.

Both gaps appear the moment an agreement is negotiated rather than adopted: a statement of work travels between parties, collects remarks, and is executed on a date the parties name.

§2

The two acts

2.1 commenting

A recorded remark on one version of one instrument, made by a person in their own name, addressable in reply. Never edited and never deleted: a withdrawal is a new row citing the remark it retires, and the original stays on the record. This follows Law I, the record corrects and does not mutate, the same way a compensating event corrects a ledger entry.

2.2 dated signing

signed_at stays what it always was, the moment the record was written. signed_on is new and holds the date the signer attests to. capacity is new and holds the capacity in which they sign: member, officer, director, or a named counterparty role.

For a member signing in the browser the two dates are the same day. For a countersigned instrument that arrives already executed they are not, and holding both is the point.

§3

User stories

Six stories, each with the condition under which it is accepted. An acceptance line that cannot be checked is not an acceptance line.

memberRead before signingI want to read what others have said about an instrument before I sign it, so that my signature is informed rather than merely collected. Accepted when an active member opens an agreement on /commons/agreements/ and sees every remark on that version, oldest first, with the name of the person who made it and the date.
memberLeave a remarkI want to leave a remark on an instrument in my own name, so that a question about a clause is on the record rather than in a chat window. Accepted when the remark is written, an agreement.commented event is recorded against the agreement, and the remark appears to every active member.
memberWithdraw a remarkI want to withdraw a remark I made, so that a point I no longer hold does not stand as if I still hold it. Accepted when a withdrawal row is written citing the original, an agreement.comment_withdrawn event is recorded, the original remark remains on the record marked withdrawn, and no one but its author can withdraw it.
officerThe attested dateI want a signature to carry the date the signer attested to, so that an instrument executed on paper before it reached the system is dated truthfully. Accepted when signing with a date records signed_on distinct from signed_at, both appear on the signature, and a date ahead of the record is refused.
officerThe capacityI want a signature to name the capacity it was made in, so that the record distinguishes a person signing as a member from the same person signing as an officer. Accepted when capacity is recorded on the signature and carried in the signature.signed event payload.
secretaryStanding before termsI want an unpresented counterparty agreement to exist on the shelf before its terms are public, so that members can see that it is in negotiation without the estate publishing what has not travelled. Accepted when an agreement row exists with settlement = anticipated, no uri, and no body text, and the surface shows it as in negotiation with no signing act offered.
§4

The migration

The deliverable is 0025_agreement_comments_and_dated_signing.sql, which carries four things and no more.

the tableagreement_comments, with its reply citation and its withdrawal citation, and a trigger holding both citations to the same instrument, so a reply cannot reach across agreements.
row securitySelect granted to active members, directors, and officers. Insert only in the caller's own name. No update policy at all, matching signatures and acknowledgements: the absence is the enforcement.
the functioncomment_on_agreement, a definer function parallel in shape to sign_agreement (0003) and acknowledge_agreement (0011), which writes the remark, writes its event, and links them atomically.
the extensionsign_agreement gains two defaulted parameters, so every existing caller keeps its previous behaviour exactly and no surface is obliged to change on the same day.

The migration is drafted and not applied. Schema is a stop-card matter under the Build Protocol, and no live schema change is made without a named human. Drafted · not applied

§6

What this does not do

The bounds, stated so that the next piece does not have to guess them.

Only agreementsNo comment on an event, a piece, or a proof. This is the agreements bed only.
No notificationNothing is sent to anyone, of any kind. A remark waits to be read.
No counterparty identityA non-member counterparty signing through the system needs an agent row and a membership question that is not answered here.
No editingBy design. A remark is corrected by a withdrawal and a new remark, per Law I.
the working agreement of this piece

An instrument that collects remarks before it is signed is an instrument that was read. The record holds what was said and who said it, and holds the date the signer names alongside the date the row was written, because a countersigned page does not arrive on the day it was executed.
