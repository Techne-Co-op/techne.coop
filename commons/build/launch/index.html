What Launch Waits On

The steward's run-through is complete and the signed-in surface stands. What remains is almost entirely adoption, attestation, and a few inputs only people can supply. One exception, added 2026-07-27: the agency train is built as far as a decision allows, and the decision it waits on is about authority, not about work. Each row below names the condition, whose act it is, how it is tested, and the review it wants before launch.

§4

What is already standing

Recorded here so the list above is read for what it is, a short one. A newcomer comes in through the front door without a meeting and is admitted by a steward's act. Agreements read and sign and acknowledge, and refuse a second signature. The directory shows the cooperative and lets each member edit only their own profile. Gatherings register, cancel, register again, and count presence where the record counts it. The board of opportunities posts, answers, and closes, and the outcome lands as an event. Programs stand designated from the intranet. The desk for Direction stands at /intranet/direct/, with the rail behind it, so a member may give the instrument work as a recorded act. Every signed-in surface sits behind one frame and one gate and speaks in the cooperative's language. The record exports, restores, and reconstructs itself from the public repository.
