Walk, Speak, Done

Fifty-two pieces of this build are delivered and waiting on one thing: a person walking each acceptance and saying what they saw. This page is that walk as a run-book. Four sittings; every card tells you where to go, what to click, and what to say. You record yourself as you walk, the transcript becomes the resolutions, and one pull request per sitting flips the marks. Nothing moves because this page exists; each piece moves when you walk it.

draft · pending the walks cites · ALM v2 · the ledger at main · the Launch-Day Walkthrough (X-21) RegenHub, LCA · Boulder · August 2026
§1

How this works

You need a browser, a phone for a few cards, and a voice recorder or live transcription. No notes, no forms: you speak the record as you walk, and the transcript is the evidence.

  1. Start recording. Say your name, the date, and which sitting you are starting.
  2. Take the cards in order. Each card is: context, numbered steps, then the say line. Do the steps exactly; do not substitute a nearby check for the stated one.
  3. Speak one verdict per card. Always the address first, then holds or fails, then one sentence of what you actually saw. Partial credit does not exist; a card that half-held, failed, and the sentence says which half.
  4. Skipping is fine, silently is not. If you defer a card (a second person is missing, a surface is down), say the address and deferred and why.
  5. Send the audio or transcript to the steward channel. The transcript is turned into per-piece resolution lines under your name and date, and one pull request per sitting flips the marks. You review that PR; the record is yours, not the transcriber’s.

Two cards defer by design to the two-person walkthrough (F-02 wholly, G-04’s last clause); their cards say so. Everything else in sittings one through four is walkable alone, today.
