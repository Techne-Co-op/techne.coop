the verification walk · X-22 · draft · tiered

Walk, Speak, Done

Fifty-two pieces of this build are delivered and waiting on one thing: a person walking each acceptance and saying what they saw. This page is that walk as a run-book, organised by criticality: four solo tiers (load-bearing truth first, polish and layout last), then a separate section for the three pieces and six attestations that cannot be walked alone. Every card tells you where to go, what to click, and what to say. You record yourself as you walk, the transcript becomes the resolutions, and one pull request per tier flips the marks. Nothing moves because this page exists; each piece moves when you walk it.

draft · pending the walks · tiered by criticality cites · ALM v2 · the ledger at main · the Launch-Day Walkthrough (X-21) RegenHub, LCA · Boulder · August 2026
§1

How this works

You need a browser, a phone for a handful of cards, and a voice recorder or live transcription. No notes, no forms: you speak the record as you walk, and the transcript is the evidence.

  1. Start recording. Say your name, the date, and which tier you are starting.
  2. Take the cards in order. Each card is: context, numbered steps, then the say line. Do the steps exactly; do not substitute a nearby check for the stated one.
  3. Speak one verdict per card. Always the address first, then holds or fails, then one sentence of what you actually saw. Partial credit does not exist; a card that half-held, failed, and the sentence says which half.
  4. Skipping is fine, silently is not. If you defer a card (a second person is missing, a surface is down), say the address and deferred and why.
  5. Send the audio or transcript to the steward channel. The transcript is turned into per-piece resolution lines under your name and date, and one pull request per tier flips the marks. You review that PR; the record is yours, not the transcriber’s.

Or walk it in the page. A member signed in to the intranet can walk the same cards at /intranet/verification/, clicking the verdict and typing the sentence instead of speaking them into a recording. The cards there are generated from this page, so the two cannot disagree; the surface holds your verdicts in your own browser, sends nothing anywhere, and writes the record of the walk for you to hand on. Everything else below is the same walk either way, and this page stays the one a person can read without signing in to anything.

Four tiers, ordered by what breaks if a card fails. Tier one is the record telling the truth about the cooperative: adoptions, refusals, the CI teeth that stop drift. Tier two is the vocabulary and instruments the estate rests on. Tier three is the working surfaces a member actually uses. Tier four is polish and layout: visible when it breaks, not load-bearing. Stop at any tier boundary and resume another day; the record moves piece by piece regardless.

Two of the tier-one groups need a second person by design (a self-response and a self-witness are exactly what those acceptances exclude) and live in a separate section below. Do not attempt them solo; walking one alone produces a verdict the record cannot use. Book them around a moment when someone else is already here, and bring the two-person walkthrough script.
