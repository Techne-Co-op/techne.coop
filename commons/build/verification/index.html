the verification walk · X-22 · draft

The Human Work Between Delivered and Verified

Fifty-one pieces of this build are delivered: merged to main, standing, and unwalked. Every one of them waits on the same input, a person walking its acceptance and saying what they saw. Nothing below is blocked on more building. This page organises that human work into five sittings with inline instructions, so a walker needs nothing but this page, a browser, and the pieces themselves. No mark moves because this page exists; each moves when its walk is walked and its resolution recorded.

§2

How to walk a piece

The method is the same for every card below, and it is deliberately small:

  1. Open the piece. The card names where the deliverable lives: a page, a schema, a workflow. Open the real thing, not a screenshot of it.
  2. Do what the acceptance sentence says. The card quotes it. If it says a page reads true against a source, read both. If it says an act lands as an event, perform the act and look for the event. Do not substitute a plausible nearby check for the stated one.
  3. Decide: held or failed. Partial credit does not exist here. If one clause of the acceptance did not hold, the piece failed its walk, and the failure is the finding.
  4. Record the resolution. One or two sentences: who walked it, the date, what was seen. This lands in the ledger as the piece’s resolution and the status moves to open · verified. A failed walk lands as a note naming what failed, and the status stays.
  5. One pull request per sitting. The walker’s confirmations from a sitting travel together; the PR flips the marks and carries the resolutions.

The steward can walk every solo card. One card in sitting two and the attestations in §8 want a second person; the Launch-Day Walkthrough is the script for that sitting and this page does not duplicate it.
