The Almanac

The plan of the build as an almanac: every piece with an address, a readiness condition, its citations into the series, and an honest mark. No dates: work waits on conditions the way sowing waits on soil, never on the calendar. The register is the build’s own, held in the Lexicon; the native vocabulary of the cooperative keeps a register of its own beside it, and the two meet without merging. Cites: ALM v2 · PRD v0.4 · BP v2 · VS v2 · AM v0.1 · the module documents: PATRONAGE, TREASURY, AGY.

Where the build stands · 2026-08-10

The steward's run-through is complete: a newcomer came in through the front door without a meeting, signed, appeared in the directory, registered and was counted present, posted and resolved on the board, and both principal Programs were designated from the intranet rather than the console. Work Standing and Guild Participation are staged as the V and P beds, drafted and not adopted, recorded so the work is legible and marked so no one mistakes a proposal for a decision.

Between those, a design sprint ran through the U bed, U-08 to U-15, giving the estate its pattern library, a public participation surface, one primary navigation, and an intranet answered to eleven minutes of the steward's own walkthrough; then U-17 to U-22 answered what a phone showed, what the frame had been claiming and not doing, what the public face still said in the retired register, and where the words on that face came from.
